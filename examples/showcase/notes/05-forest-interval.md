# 05 Forest Interval Design Notes

## Visual Strategy
Use horizontal intervals when ranking uncertainty-aware effects. The zero line is the perceptual anchor, while alternating bands make long labels easy to scan.

The intended conclusion is: Timing and spread factors have the strongest positive effects, while stress exposure remains negative.

## Use When
Use when each row has an estimate plus uncertainty and the reader must compare against a baseline or zero.

## Avoid
Avoid when intervals are not comparable or when the baseline has no interpretive meaning.
