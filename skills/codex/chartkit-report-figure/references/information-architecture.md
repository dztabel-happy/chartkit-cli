# Information Architecture

Every panel must answer a distinct evidence question.

Good panel maps:

- `(a)` sample space or primary comparison
- `(b)` score distribution or uncertainty
- `(c)` structure matrix or feature relationship
- `(d)` external agreement or calibration
- `(e)` multi-metric tradeoff
- `(f)` dynamics or robustness

Bad panel maps:

- repeated accuracy bars
- workflow as the whole figure
- metric cards as evidence
- explanatory paragraphs inside panels

The caption/report body carries prose. The figure carries evidence.
